Counter-Strike: Football — 2v2 to 5v5 competitive FPS with VibeHub online multiplayer.

Two football fan teams fight over the graffiti bomb: Attackers must plant it in the Defenders' sanctuary, Defenders must stop them and defuse. Win rounds to win the match.

Features:
- Full online multiplayer over VibeHub P2P: host-authoritative simulation in your browser, snapshot interpolation, reliable events (kills, plants, round flow), bots fill empty slots with pathfinding AI
- VibeHub account: login, cloud-synced settings, personal stats (kills/deaths/wins), global leaderboard
- CS-style economy: buy weapons and grenades each round (AK-47, M4A4, AWP, pistols, flash/smoke/molotov/HE/decoy, kevlar, defuse kit)
- 3D world on three.js, pointer-lock controls, radar, buy menu, scoreboard, round damage feedback
- Map: Dust-like default arena with plant sites and buy zones

Play: login with your VibeHub account, create a room or quick match, invite friends by sharing the room id (password-protected rooms supported).
